From the original review

God of War for the PlayStation 4 is a testiment of how single player games are not dead. In a world where developers are scared to sink money into a single player narrative with no micro transactions and no paywalls, God of War stands as one of the best games of 2018. With out a doubt it shows the games industry as a whole that players still crave the single player games that are well crafted that tell a great story.
