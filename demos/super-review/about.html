BEHIND THE ARCHIVE

Built for the love of games.

Super Review began as Mike McDougall’s gaming editorial student project in 2018. This redesign keeps the original game collection and gives it a clearer, more usable home.

A new look. The original collection.

Dark surfaces, red accents, rounded imagery, and a responsive editorial grid make the game artwork the focus. Reading pages, navigation, and search connect the collection.

What “archive” means here

These are historical student-project selections, not current gaming news. Original writing is labeled. Where a homepage story never had a complete article, its page preserves the images and context rather than inventing a review, byline, or score.

About the imagery

Game images and the original Super Review logo are retained from the source project. Their rights remain with the respective owners. This is an independent portfolio concept with no affiliation to the games or publishers featured.
